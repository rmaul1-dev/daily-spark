# Daily Spark ✨

A few minutes a day of bite-sized learning for the whole family. Pick a topic, swipe through a short pack of idea cards, think, take a quick check, and teach it back at dinner.

**Open it:** https://rmaul1-dev.github.io/daily-spark/

On an iPhone or iPad, open the link in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, and it runs full-screen like an app. Packs you've opened (and the ones it downloads in the background) work offline.

- **Topics:** History · Science · Tech & AI · Politics & Civics · Money & Business · Space · Human Body & Health · Big Ideas
- **Three levels:** Kid, Teen and Grown-up versions of each subject
- **Family topic of the day:** everyone learns the same topic at their own level, with a dinner question to talk about
- **How do we know?** Every pack lists the sources behind its facts
- Streaks, saved cards, history, spaced review of missed questions, and a parent view

Progress is stored only in the browser on each device (localStorage). No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

All lesson text, quizzes and artwork are original. Lessons live as plain JSON in `content/` (`index.json` + `packs/`), so new packs can be added without rebuilding the app.
