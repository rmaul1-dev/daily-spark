# Daily Spark ✨

A few minutes a day of bite-sized learning for the whole family. Pick a topic, swipe through a short pack of idea cards, think, take a quick check, and teach it back at dinner.

**Open it:** https://rmaul1-dev.github.io/daily-spark/

On an iPhone or iPad, open the link in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, and it runs full-screen like an app. Packs you've opened (and the ones it downloads in the background) work offline.

- **Topics:** History · Science · Tech & AI · Politics & Civics · Money & Business · Space · Human Body & Health · Big Ideas
- **Three levels:** Kid, Teen and Grown-up versions of each subject
- **Family topic of the day:** everyone learns the same topic at their own level, with a dinner question to talk about
- **Watch:** most packs include one short, hand-checked learning video (YouTube, played inside the app in a sandboxed privacy-enhanced player; it can be turned off per learner in the parent view and is never required to finish a pack)
- **How do we know?** Every pack lists the sources behind its facts
- Streaks, saved cards, history, spaced review of missed questions, and a parent view

Progress is stored only in the browser on each device (localStorage). No accounts, no ads, no tracking in the app. The only outside requests are a source link you tap and, on a Watch card, the video thumbnail and (after you tap Play) YouTube's privacy-enhanced player from youtube-nocookie.com, which YouTube runs under its own terms (it may show an ad on some videos).

All lesson text, quizzes and artwork are original; videos belong to their creators and are embedded with YouTube's player. Lessons live as plain JSON in `content/` (`index.json` + `packs/`), so new packs can be added without rebuilding the app.
